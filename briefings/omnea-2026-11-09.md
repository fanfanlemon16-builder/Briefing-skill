# Omnea — Briefing
*As of 9 Nov 2026 · AI procurement platform · AI-application (built on rented models)*
*Role in view: **Founding Enterprise Account Executive – Singapore***

## TL;DR
- Software that gives employees one "front door" for buying things (software, services, suppliers). It routes each request through legal, security and finance approvals, then tracks suppliers and renewals.
- **Funding:** $50M Series B (Sep 2025) led by **Insight Partners and Khosla**, with Accel; $75M+ raised. 🟡 A further undisclosed round reported Mar 2026.
- **Growth:** revenue up ~5x in the year to Sep 2025; 🟡 ~$1M → $10M ARR in 18 months. Named the #1 UK startup (Startups 100, 2026).
- **Customers:** Spotify, Wise, Adyen, MongoDB, T. Rowe Price, S&P Global, Just Eat, Albertsons, Adecco.
- **APAC:** just starting. It's hiring an **APAC GM in Sydney** and a **first AE in Singapore** (remote until there's an office). No Asian customers named.

## What it does & how it makes money
- **Products:** procurement intake (one request form for everything), approval workflows across teams, supplier onboarding and risk checks, renewals tracking, AI agents, and an MCP server so tools like Claude and ChatGPT can query supplier data.
- **Model:** annual enterprise SaaS subscriptions (pricing private).
- **Biggest stream:** enterprise subscriptions, mostly in Europe and North America.
- **Buyer:** CFO, procurement lead, often IT/security as co-buyers.

## Competitive landscape

**Market direction:** a hot category, with "intake and orchestration" sitting on top of old procurement suites. The leaders are well funded, so this becomes a sales race.

## Moat *(analysis)*
- **Tech:** owns workflow, integrations (ERP, SSO, Slack/Teams) and supplier data. AI runs on rented LLMs.
- **Lock-in:** moderate. Once every purchase runs through it, switching is painful, but the category is young.
- **Rating: Moderate–Thin.** Strong logos and momentum; product differences against Zip and others can be copied.

## The role: Founding Enterprise AE – Singapore
- **Posting:** first enterprise AE in Singapore; remote at first; senior level; 3–7 years of B2B SaaS sales with a record of closing enterprise deals in Singapore.
- **What it really is:** a founding seller in a new region. You'd be selling to CFOs and procurement leaders at large regional companies (banks, telcos, conglomerates, tech) with no local references yet. Expect 6–12 month cycles.
- **Green flags:** strong investors and logos; fast growth; a founding title is a real career step.
- **Red flags:** no local office, customers or GM yet. You'd be selling Europe/US references into Asia.

## Questions to ask
- Is the APAC GM hired? Who would I report to now, and after they're hired?
- Do you have any APAC customers or pipeline today, including subsidiaries of global customers (e.g. Spotify, Adyen, S&P Global in Singapore)?
- What's the ramp and quota for year one, and is it ARR or bookings based?
- What pre-sales, implementation and support will be in my time zone?
- How does Omnea handle data residency for Singapore/APAC clients?
- Revenue and growth now, and runway after the Series B?
- Equity: grant priced off which valuation? Vesting and exercise window?
