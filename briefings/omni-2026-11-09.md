# Omni — Briefing
*As of 9 Nov 2026 · AI analytics / business intelligence (omni.co) · AI-application (rented LLMs on its own semantic layer) · Not OmniSci, AppOmni, Omnicom Omni or Omni HR*
*Role in view: **Enterprise Account Executive – Singapore***

## TL;DR
- A business-intelligence platform (dashboards, analysis, embedded analytics) built by the team behind **Looker**. Its core is a "semantic layer": one shared definition of company metrics that both people and AI agents can trust.
- **Funding:** $120M Series C at **$1.5B** (Apr 2026), led by ICONIQ, including a $30M employee tender. That's up from $650M in Mar 2025.
- **Growth:** ~4x year on year; **profitable for the first time in Mar 2026** (reported). It had projected $30M ARR for end-2025.
- **Moat: Moderate.** Strong founder pedigree and a semantic layer it owns, but it competes with Microsoft, Google, Salesforce and the data platforms.
- **APAC:** a real team in **Sydney** with a VP of APAC. The **Singapore AE is the first hire on the ground**, though the listing appears only on aggregators. Verify it.

## What it does & how it makes money
- **Products:** dashboards and spreadsheet-style analysis; AI that answers questions in plain language; embedded analytics for SaaS companies to put inside their own products (boosted by buying **Explo**, Oct 2025). The semantic layer sits on Snowflake, Databricks, BigQuery and similar.
- **Model:** annual SaaS subscriptions; embedded-analytics contracts.
- **Biggest stream:** core BI subscriptions from mid-size and enterprise companies (500–5,000 employees per its job posts).
- **Customers:** BambooHR, Guitar Center, Checkr, Mercury, Pendo, Cribl.

## Competitive landscape
| Layer | Competitor | Where they beat Omni | Where Omni wins |
|---|---|---|---|
| BI giants | **Power BI** (Microsoft), **Tableau** (Salesforce), **Looker** (Google) | Installed base; bundled with Microsoft 365 / Salesforce / Google Cloud | Modern UX, faster setup, Looker team without Google lock-in, AI on a trusted semantic layer |
| Modern BI | **Sigma**, ThoughtSpot, Hex | Sigma: bigger and spreadsheet-native; Hex: data-science teams | Governance and semantic layer; Looker migrations |
| Data platforms | Snowflake, Databricks (AI/BI, Genie) | Own the data; "free" built-in BI | Works across platforms; richer modelling |
| Embedded | Explo (now Omni), Luzmo, Sisense | — | Combined BI + embedded |

**Frenemies:** Snowflake and Databricks are partners and growing competitors.
**Market direction:** BI is being rebuilt around AI agents. Whoever owns trusted metric definitions (the semantic layer) wins, which is exactly Omni's pitch.

## Moat *(analysis)*
- **Owned:** semantic layer, query engine, UX; brand among ex-Looker users and data teams.
- **Rented:** LLMs for AI features; customers' data platforms.
- **Lock-in:** high once metrics are modelled in Omni.
- **Rating: Moderate.** Good product and timing; big bundling threat from Microsoft and the data platforms.
