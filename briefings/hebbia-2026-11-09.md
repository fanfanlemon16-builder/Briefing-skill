# Hebbia — Briefing
*As of 9 Nov 2026 · AI research and analysis for finance (banks, asset managers, PE, law) · AI-application (rented LLMs, own retrieval/orchestration)*
*Role in view: **Regional Sales Lead – APAC** (Singapore)*

## TL;DR
- **Matrix** lets analysts at banks and funds ask questions across thousands of documents (filings, data rooms, research) and get answers in a spreadsheet-like grid with citations. It's now adding agents and integrations.
- **ARR ~$48–50M** (Aug–Sep 2026, estimates / The Information), up from $13M in Jul 2024.
- **Funding:** $130M Series B at **$700M** (Jul 2024), led by a16z. **No new round since.** Secondary-market price is ~$671M (Forge, Oct 2026), slightly below the last round.
- **Keystone win:** **Morgan Stanley** rolled it out to ~5,000 bankers after a six-month bake-off against Rogo. Customers include ~1/3 of the largest asset managers.
- **APAC:** none yet. The Regional Sales Lead is the **first dedicated hire in the region**, Singapore-based, with a path to Regional Director.

## What it does & how it makes money
- **Products:** Matrix (Matrix 2.0, Aug 2026), agents and 30+ "Skills" for bankers, lawyers and investors, an email agent, and a "headless" API/MCP so it works inside other tools. Integrations include **Databricks, Datasite** (deal data rooms), **Octus** (credit research), and **Microsoft Copilot**.
- **Model:** enterprise subscriptions, usually priced per seat and by firm, with large firm-wide deals (e.g. Morgan Stanley).
- **Biggest stream:** enterprise contracts with investment banks, asset managers and PE firms in the US and UK.

## Competitive landscape
| Layer | Competitor | Where they beat Hebbia | Where Hebbia wins |
|---|---|---|---|
| Finance AI pure-plays | **Rogo** | Banker-specific workflows, aggressive growth | Won Morgan Stanley head-to-head; asset-manager depth |
| Research platforms | **AlphaSense** (+Tegus) | Proprietary content (expert calls, broker research), huge base | Works on the firm's own documents at scale |
| Data incumbents | Bloomberg, LSEG, FactSet, S&P AI tools | Own the data and terminals | Neutral across data sources |
| Frontier labs | **Anthropic** (Claude for Financial Services), **OpenAI**, Microsoft Copilot | Own the models; bundled pricing; Copilot already on every desk | Finance-specific accuracy, citations, workflows |
| Legal | Harvey | Legal depth | Finance-first |

**Frenemies:** OpenAI and Microsoft (suppliers and distribution, and also competitors), Anthropic.
**Market direction:** frontier labs are moving straight into financial services, which squeezes app-layer players unless they own workflows and data integrations.

## The role: Regional Sales Lead – APAC
- **Posting:** Singapore-based (or relocate), travel to Hong Kong, Tokyo, Sydney, Mumbai; first dedicated hire in the region; path to Regional Director with your own team. Last published ~12 Jul 2026, so it may be filled.
- **Asks for:** 7+ years of enterprise software sales, 3+ selling into financial services.
- **What it really is:** land-and-expand into APAC desks of global banks, asset managers, sovereign funds (GIC, Temasek-type) and PE. Big-ticket deals with security reviews and pilots; cycles 6–12 months.
- **Green flags:** clearest career step of the four (path to Regional Director); strongest references (Morgan Stanley); natural pipeline from global clients.
- **Red flags:** funding gap since 2024; intense culture; frontier labs competing for the same buyers.
