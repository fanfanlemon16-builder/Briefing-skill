# Velocity — Briefing
*As of 9 Nov 2026 · Stablecoin treasury and settlement for enterprises · Not AI · Not Velo Labs (Singapore), VelocityEHS or Velocity Global*
*Role in view: **Enterprise Sales Director – APAC** (Singapore or Hong Kong)*

## TL;DR
- Builds software that lets large companies and financial institutions **hold, move and settle money in stablecoins** (digital dollars), connected to their banks, custody and compliance systems.
- **Very young:** out of stealth May 2025. Series A **$38M** (Jul 2026, led by Dragonfly and FirstMark) + **$10M** extension (Sep 2026) with Visa, Circle and Ripple.
- **Revenue not disclosed.** Operates in the US, parts of Europe and Australia.
- **Moat: thin for now.** It's an early race against much bigger players (Stripe's Bridge, BVNK, Circle's own network).
- **APAC role:** one of the first hires in the region, posted for both Singapore and Hong Kong, listed at SGD 200–320K. The big unknown is which Asian licences it holds.

## What it does & how it makes money
A "stablecoin payment account" for businesses. Companies can receive, hold and pay out stablecoins (e.g. USDC), convert to and from local currency through banking partners, and settle cross-border payments in minutes instead of days. It plans to add custody and yield on balances.

**Revenue model** *(analysis — not disclosed)*: likely fees on payment and conversion volume (basis points), platform/account fees, and a share of yield on balances. **Biggest revenue stream:** unknown. Ask in interviews.

## Competitive landscape
| Layer | Competitor | Where they beat Velocity | Where Velocity could win |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stablecoin infrastructure | **Bridge (Stripe)**, **BVNK**, Zerohash, Conduit, Paxos | Years ahead, licences in many places, big customer bases, Stripe distribution | Treasury focus for large enterprises; investor network |
| Issuer networks | **Circle** (payments network), Ripple | Own the stablecoins and the rails | Neutral across issuers |
| Banks | JPMorgan (Kinexys), Standard Chartered (HKD stablecoin with Animoca) | Balance sheets, existing corporate relationships | Speed, multi-stablecoin |
| Asia-local | 🇭🇰 Reap (bought by Kraken's Payward for $600M), HashKey; 🇸🇬 StraitsX, dtcpay; Ant Group (applying for HK/SG licences) | Local licences and corporate networks | Global enterprise accounts |

**Frenemies:** Circle, Ripple, Visa and Coinbase are investors *and* run competing products.
**Market direction:** booming. Singapore and Hong Kong are the #2 and #3 stablecoin hubs after the US, but licensing is the gate. Hong Kong's issuer regime is live, with dozens of applications under review.

## Moat *(analysis)*
- **Tech:** owned orchestration layer connecting stablecoins, banks and compliance. Not proprietary in a hard-to-copy sense.
- **What could become a moat:** licences, banking partners in each corridor, and Fortune-500 references. None are proven yet.
- **Rating: Thin.** Normal for an 18-month-old company. The bet is on the team and investors.

## The role: Enterprise Sales Director – APAC
- **Posting:** Singapore (SGD 200–320K, unclear if base or on-target earnings) and Hong Kong versions, posted ~24 Sep 2026; may be filled.
- **Asks for:** 6–10+ years of enterprise sales/BD/partnerships, mostly quota-carrying closing roles, and an established APAC payments/fintech network.
- **What it really is:** a founding regional seller. Expect to sell to treasurers, payment companies and banks with long, compliance-heavy cycles, and to help decide which licences and partners to get first.
- **Green flags:** strong investors; hot market; seniority and comp are real.
- **Red flags:** no visible licences or Asian customers; two parallel postings may mean they're unsure where to base the region.

## Questions to ask
- Which licences do you hold or have applied for (MAS, HKMA/SFC, AUSTRAC)? What can I legally sell in Singapore and Hong Kong today?
- Is there revenue today? What are the top use cases and average deal size?
- Why Singapore *and* Hong Kong? Will both hires exist, and who leads the region?
- Who would I report to, and what support (solutions, compliance, legal) is in-region?
- How does quota work in year one with no pipeline — ramp, draw, or target based on deals or partners?
- Equity: what was the Series A price? Option type, vesting, exercise window?
