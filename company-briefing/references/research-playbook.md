# Research playbook

Run these as one parallel batch. Replace `{Co}` with the company name and `{YYYY}` with the current year.

| # | Section | Query |
|---|---------|-------|
| 1 | Funding | `{Co} funding round {YYYY} valuation lead investor` |
| 2 | Funding | `{Co} tender offer OR secondary valuation {YYYY}` |
| 3 | Revenue | `{Co} ARR revenue {YYYY}` |
| 4 | Business model | `{Co} pricing business model enterprise vs self-serve revenue split` |
| 5 | Products / what it does | `{Co} products launch {YYYY}` |
| 6 | HQ & founders | `{Co} headquarters founders CEO` |
| 7 | Moat / competition | `{Co} competitors vs` and `{Co} moat` |
| 8 | Asia overview | `{Co} Asia Pacific expansion office` |
| 9 | Japan / Korea | `{Co} Japan partnership customers` · `{Co} Korea launch` |
| 10 | India | `{Co} India revenue customers partners` |
| 11 | SEA / ANZ / China | `{Co} Singapore Australia` · `{Co} China` |
| 12 | Latest news | `{Co} news {current month} {YYYY}` |

## Where to look first

- **Company newsroom/blog** — funding posts, market launches, "we're expanding into X".
- **Partner press releases** — often the only source for Asia deals (search in the local language if needed, e.g. Japanese company names).
- **Tier-1 press** — Bloomberg, Reuters, TechCrunch, The Information, FT; regional: Nikkei, Business Today/Economic Times (India), Korea Herald, Sifted (Europe), DealStreetAsia.
- **Data aggregators** — Sacra, Crunchbase, PitchBook, CB Insights, Dealroom. Use for cross-checks; label as estimates.
- **Careers page / job boards** — reveal which Asian cities they're staffing (a 🔍 signal only).

## Follow-ups

If a section is thin after the first batch, run one more targeted search (try `extended` mode) before marking it "Not disclosed".
